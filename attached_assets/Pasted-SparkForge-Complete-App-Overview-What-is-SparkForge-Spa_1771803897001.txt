SparkForge - Complete App Overview
What is SparkForge?
SparkForge is an AI-powered platform that turns conversations into complete digital product businesses. It enables creators to ideate, build, brand, package, and sell digital products through natural AI-assisted conversations—from initial concept to final sale.

🎯 Core Concept
Instead of manually creating digital products, users simply chat with an AI assistant that guides them through the entire process:

Product creation
Content generation
Brand identity design
Storefront setup
Sales and analytics
🛍️ Digital Products You Can Create
1. E-books
Professional e-books with AI-generated content, covers, and formatting
Export to PDF, ePub, and MOBI formats
Examples: Business Guides, Recipe Books, Travel Guides, Self-Help, Novels
Time: 30-60 minutes
Difficulty: Beginner
2. Workbooks
Interactive workbooks with exercises, worksheets, and hands-on activities
Built-in completion tracking and progress monitoring
Examples: Training Workbooks, Skill-Building Exercises, Assessment Tools
Time: 45-75 minutes
Difficulty: Beginner
3. Online Courses
Comprehensive courses with video outlines, scripts, and slides
Lesson-by-lesson structure with interactive elements
Progress tracking and completion certificates
Examples: Programming, Marketing, Design, Photography, Cooking
Time: 2-4 hours
Difficulty: Intermediate
4. Interactive Planners
Dynamic planners with task management (Kanban boards)
Habit tracking with streak counters

Feature: Tasks can be scheduled with start dates
